      Use your device location, search RTS stop names, enter coordinates, or select points on the map.

      LeaveNow checks up to six nearby stops at each end and matches live predictions by the same trip and vehicle. It only recommends a bus if your destination stop comes after your boarding stop and you have time to walk there.

      Walking times are approximate: straight-line distance with a 25% allowance, at 80 meters per minute, plus a two-minute boarding buffer. The dotted map line is a connection guide, not a street route. Check crossings and sidewalks.

      This version finds direct buses in the current live window. Transfers, future timetables, and turn-by-turn walking routes are not included. RTS predictions can change.

      Coordinates go to this app’s server to find stops. The map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap. Locations are not saved by the app.
